①
EU Responsible Person
Every product must have an "in-EU" Responsible Person (seller / EU importer / EU-based agent). No Rep = cannot be sold legally.
②
Safety warnings in the local language
Warnings must be in the official language of each target country: DE for Germany, FR for France, PL for Poland, etc. English alone is not compliant.
③
15-day market-surveillance reporting
Any safety incident must be reported to the member state's market-surveillance authority within 15 days. CrossAIO helps you set reminders and generates the report template.
④
EC Declaration of Conformity (DoC)
The technical DoC must be available upon request from the platform or the authority; CrossAIO provides a fillable template.
