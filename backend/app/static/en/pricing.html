Billing FAQ

About payments, quota and compliance responsibility

Why isn't the GPSR compliance block on the free plan?
The free plan does a check (reports only: what you're missing, what's non-compliant), while paid plans give you the cure (generates the full paste-ready compliance block). EU 2023/988 is already enforced, and non-compliance gets you delisted. Starter at €29/mo buys you "insurance against delisting".
How does annual billing work? Can I get a refund?
Annual billing is charged once at 80% of the monthly price (e.g. Starter annual is €276, equal to €23/mo), cancel anytime, prorated refund for unused months. Monthly billing charges month by month; after cancellation the current period stays active until it ends.
Whose EU Responsible Person (EU Rep) is it? Does CrossAIO provide one?
The EU Rep must be you or an entity within the EU that you appoint — it is a legal role that no software can replace. CrossAIO provides an EU Rep registry: store your real details (or those of the EU Rep service you purchased) in the registry, and they are automatically embedded into every compliance block — no more hand-filling placeholders.
How are calls counted?
Each successful API call (generation / check / customer service / ads) counts as 1; failed calls don't. Multi-platform matrix counts per platform (one run across 5 platforms = 5 calls), and a failed single platform is deducted automatically and counted platform by platform.
What's the difference between platform-native rule blocks and regular compliance blocks?
Regular compliance blocks generate local-language safety warnings based on the countries a platform covers; platform-native blocks are generated directly from that platform's own compliance policy: Allegro Polish header + UOKiK authority, eMAG Romanian + ANPC, Cdiscount French (Loi Toubon mandatory) + DGCCRF, and so on — the wording aligns with platform enforcement language, so backfilled listings pass review more reliably.
What payment methods are supported?
Two channels: Stripe (Visa/Mastercard/Amex international cards, mainstream in Europe) + PayPal (covers buyers who prefer PayPal, especially the German market). Either one completes the subscription; cancellation takes effect immediately. Before launch, this runs in local demo mode with a one-click simulated upgrade to try all features.
