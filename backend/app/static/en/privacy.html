1. Data we collect

  • Account data: registration email, subscription plan, usage statistics.
  • Platform credentials: e-commerce platform credentials you authorize through the credential center (e.g. Amazon SP-API, eBay OAuth). Credentials are stored encrypted with AES-256 and used only to call the corresponding platform APIs on your behalf.

  • Technical data: login IP, device and browser information, necessary cookies.

2. How we use data

  • To provide and improve AI generation, GPSR compliance checks, the unified inbox, Listing monitoring and other features;
  • To read from and write to the e-commerce platforms you connect, within the scope of your authorization;
  • Billing, risk control and customer support.

3. Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

Processing is based on: contract performance (providing the service to you), legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention), and your consent (non-essential cookies and marketing).

4. Data sharing and third parties

  • E-commerce platforms (Amazon / eBay etc.): called only within the scope of your authorization;
  • Payment processors (Stripe / PayPal): used only for charging, card numbers are not stored;
  • Cloud providers (hosting and object storage): processed under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained for the lifetime of the account and deleted within 30 days after closure. Platform credential ciphertexts are deleted immediately after you disconnect. You may request export or deletion at any time via [[privacy@your-company.com]] (GDPR Art. 15/17).

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. To exercise your rights, contact the email above; we respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use necessary cookies to maintain login, and (with your consent) analytics cookies. You can manage preferences via the page cookie banner.

8. Contact and complaints

For any questions, contact [[privacy@your-company.com]]; you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your EU member state.
