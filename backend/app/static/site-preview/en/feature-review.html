02 / Five-step flow

Changes from original sound to executable

Each suggestion can correspond to a real comment, and it is not made out of thin air.
STEP 01

Original sound clustering

Group the comments by topic and give proportions and representative sentences.

STEP 02

Compliance filtering

Eliminate untouchable writing methods such as inducing positive reviews, cashback in exchange for reviews, etc.

STEP 03

Five point rewrite

Change the title and five points for the two topics with the highest proportion.

STEP 04

Semantic scoring

Run it once before and after the change to quantify the difference rather than relying on feelings.

STEP 05

deliver

Give a list of changes and expected impact.

09 / FAQ

Four questions about reputation repair

Will negative reviews be deleted?

Won't. Only analysis and rewriting, no evaluation intervention.

How many samples are needed?

More than 50 items can identify stable themes, too few can only give direction.

Will it be effective after the modification?

There is no guarantee, so there is a retest and rollback mechanism.

Can you analyze competing products?

Yes, use public comments and do not capture private domain data.
