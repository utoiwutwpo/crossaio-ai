09 / FAQ

The four things you most want to ask when something happens

Is the appeal letter a template?

It's a skeleton, not a fill-in-the-blank. The reason code, evidence number, and time limit are all written according to the facts of the case.

What happens if there is not enough evidence?

It will be clearly stated which items are missing and a list of alternative materials will be given instead of just making up.

What should I do if the platform doesn’t reply?

Upgrade according to the deadline, and the second submission will bring the previous receipt number.

Will recovery be guaranteed?

Not guaranteed. Just ensure that the materials are complete, consistent in caliber, and submitted on time.
