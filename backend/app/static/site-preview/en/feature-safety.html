02 / Five-step flow

Identify → Retain certificate → Transfer

There is no automatic reply in the risk control process. This is our deliberate choice.
STEP 01

Semantic recognition

Determine risk types based on utterance characteristics and context, not just keywords.

STEP 02

Evidence retention

The original text, time, platform, and session ID are archived together.

STEP 03

hierarchical allocation

Assign to the corresponding person in charge according to severity.

STEP 04

Manual reply

Suggested wording is given, and it is up to people to post it.

STEP 05

Review

Weekly summary type distribution and processing time.

09 / FAQ

Four must-answer questions for risk interception

Will it accidentally injure normal customers?

Manual review will be done, and false alarms can be released with one click and the reasons will be recorded.

Can evidence be used to appeal against the platform?

Yes, with time and session ID, ready to export.

Why not auto-reply?

In risky scenarios, automatic replies will increase responsibility, and it is safer to default to not moving.

Can I access my own customer service system?

Yes, message callback is performed without replacing the original system.
