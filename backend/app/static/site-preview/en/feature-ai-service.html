Not all messages should be answered by AI.
STEP 01

Unified receipt

Messages from each platform are collected into a list, sorted by urgency.

STEP 02

risk classification

Threats, claims, and legal related issues are directly transferred to labor.

STEP 03

Draft generation

Generate a response according to the tone of the platform, with an Art.50 disclosure sentence.

STEP 04

disclosure verification

Manual takeover

09 / FAQ

Four boundary issues of AI customer service

Will AI answer random questions on its own?

No, the draft must have a source and be manually reviewed by default before being posted.

Can the disclosure sentence be changed?

You can change the copywriting, but you cannot delete it; if you delete it, the verification will not pass.

Will the transfer be slow?

The transfer is contextual, so customer service doesn’t need to ask again.

What languages ​​are supported?

Site language priority, 8 languages ​​can be switched.
