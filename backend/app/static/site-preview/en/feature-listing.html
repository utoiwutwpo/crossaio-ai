02 / Five-step flow

Selling points first, then copywriting, and finally verification

The order cannot be reversed. Writing beautifully first and then cutting it off is the most common way to overturn it.
STEP 01

Selling point extraction

Extract 3–5 verifiable selling points from your manuscript, minus the adjective stuffing.

STEP 02

Platform rearrangement

Rearrange the title structure according to platform search habits and do not use the same template.

STEP 03

Word count and banned words

Hard verification is performed on a platform-by-platform basis. Out-of-bounds errors are directly marked in red and given alternative writing methods.

STEP 04

A/B version

One page focuses on keywords, and the other page focuses on conversions. It’s up to you to choose.

STEP 05

Export

09 / FAQ

Four questions about multi-platform copywriting

Will it be written with the same content?

No, the platform structure and tone are different and will be rewritten platform by platform.

Can the brand tone be preserved?

Yes, after locking the tone rules, rewrite according to the rules.

Will it be truncated if the word count exceeds the limit?

Don’t truncate, give two shorter ways of writing to choose from.

How to choose between A/B version?

Run with small traffic first, then use clicks and conversions to backfill, rather than relying on feeling.
