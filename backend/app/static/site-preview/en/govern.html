09 / FAQ

Four things you must answer before handing over your account

Can the traces be deleted?

cannot. Only correction records can be added, and the original records will be retained.

Who will see the credentials?

Only authorized roles can call it, and the call itself leaves traces and the clear text is not issued.

Can the report be provided to the outside world?

Can export PDF/CSV, including detailed appendices, which can be directly submitted to audit.

Why can only 1 platform be left unattended?

The rest of the platforms do not have public messaging interfaces or require qualifications, so it cannot be said that they can be automated.
