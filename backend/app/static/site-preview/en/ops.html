09 / FAQ

Four things you care about most in daily operations

Will the inspection disturb the platform?

The main interface is read-only, and write operations are authorized individually and left traces one by one.

Are there too many exceptions?

Grading: red for processing on the same day, yellow for confirmation this week, and green for recording only to avoid noise.

Can I inspect only some platforms?

Yes, you can select by store and site. Unselected platforms will be marked as not covered in the report.

Can the daily report be customized?

Yes, templates can be saved according to platform, responsible person, and SKU dimension combinations.
