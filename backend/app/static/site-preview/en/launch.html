09 / FAQ

The four most frequently asked questions before putting it on the shelves

Can it be changed after blocking?

able. After patching, re-verify, only the affected ones will be re-run, not the entire batch.

Is the EAN check digit calculated automatically?

Yes, count the 13th position according to GS1 rules. If the calculation is wrong, it will be marked in red and the correct value will be given.

Can the submission package be uploaded directly?

Passed items can be uploaded directly; blocked items are not included in the package to avoid being mixed in and returned in batches.

Do I need to change the format of the mother table?

Need not. Fill in according to the existing template, and field mapping is completed during import.
